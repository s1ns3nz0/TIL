# 1. LanguageCube 2일차
## 1) 수동태(Passive Voice)를 사용하는 이유
- 행동을 당하는 대상을 강조하고 싶을 때
- 행동을 한 주체가 누구인지 모르거나 중요하지 않은 상황일 때
- 누군가를 직접적으로 비난하는 것을 피하고 공손하게 표현하기 위해서
## 2) 상황별 주요 표현
- Discussing career and Job Market
    + I'm considering working abroad in the future.
    + AI will likely replace some jobs, but it will also create new jobs
    + The housing cost in Seoul is a major concern for many people
    + Most of the big tech companies are based in the US
- Using Passive Voice in Business Contexts
    + The meeting agenda has been sent to all participants
    + Staff turnover can be reduced if employees are paid more
    + I'm always expected to give my opinion during meetings
    + The bank account was frozen by police
## 3) 1:1 교정 노트
- It's always expected that I'll share my opinions in team meetings
- Frozen bank accounts prevent the owner from conducting economic activities
- When a bank account is frozen, the owner cannot perform any economic activities
- I have some concerns about the future of Korea's economy.
- From my perspective. Korea's economic outlook isn't very promising
